About Hadi Capish

Serious academic guidance, delivered with clarity and calm.

International-school experience meets examiner insight.

Hadi is an experienced international-school educator with more than ten years of teaching experience. He holds a BSc in Physics and an MA in Education and teaches Mathematics and Physics within the IB and IGCSE curricula.

His experience includes serving as Head of Mathematics, CAS Coordinator, IB Diploma Coordinator and Extended Essay Coordinator. As an IB Physics HL examiner and Physics Internal Assessment moderator, he understands how conceptual knowledge, structured reasoning and assessment expectations connect.

Lessons focus on conceptual understanding, problem-solving, examination technique, time management, learning strategies, wellbeing and resilience.
